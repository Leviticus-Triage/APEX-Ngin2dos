SUMMARY
-------
CVE-2026-49975 (commit 47d3100b25) correctly mitigates the empty cookie-crumb
HPACK amplification path. I have verified that on httpd 2.4.68 with mod_http2
2.0.41, a different cookie-based chain — fat dynamic-table cookie (4058 bytes)
plus indexed references (95/stream, under LimitRequestFields) scaled across
connections and streams — still causes multi-gigabyte worker memory growth and
service degradation from a single client with low wire volume.

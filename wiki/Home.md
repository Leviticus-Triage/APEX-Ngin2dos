# APEX Ngin2dos Wiki

Welcome to the **APEX Ngin2dos** research wiki — companion to the [main repository](https://github.com/Leviticus-Triage/APEX-Ngin2dos).

## What is this?

A multi-variant HTTP/2 HPACK bomb benchmark harness for **authorized** security research. It extends [califio/publications](https://github.com/califio/publications) PoCs with scaled attacks, cookie variants, IIS orchestration, MCP integration, and lab replay environments.
